# Storyboard (75 s)

Persistent actors: the three question cards (tray → phone notification) and the developer's chair.
Signature transformations:
1. A butler's paper question card flies across the room and becomes an iPhone notification.
2. "ALL DONE, SIR." gets inspected, stamped NOT DONE, then BACK TO WORK, and the butler marches off to type.
3. The empty chair spins alone after the developer sprints out into daylight.
