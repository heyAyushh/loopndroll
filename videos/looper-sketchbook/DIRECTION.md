# "The Unbroken Line" — v3 treatment (90s, 48 bars at 128 BPM)

The story is unchanged (McKee spine: see `../looper-film/DIRECTION.md`). A developer must ship by 09:00. Every agent stops and
asks "continue?", so they become the loop, typing "y" all night. At the crisis they hand the loop to
Looper and leave the desk. Dawn: shipped.

What changes is the **telling**. There are no rules and no "3D film"; it is a sketchbook that won't stop drawing itself.

## The one idea that carries everything

**The pencil lifting is the Stop. Looper is a line that never lifts.**

- When an agent stops, the drawing stops too. The page freezes half-drawn, then un-draws back toward paper.
  The music tape-stops at the same moment.
- Every "y" makes the hand start again, and each time the drawing comes back **worse**: shakier, smudged, over-erased.
  Fatigue is shown through the quality of the drawing itself.
- At the crisis choice, a **single continuous lavender ink line** starts at the glass orb and
  redraws the world (the room, the person, the tracks) without ever lifting. This is continuous-line art as the product.

## Genres collide, each with a reason

| Genre | Used for | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphite noir storyboard | The human night | Tired, intimate, grey: a person drawn by hand |
| Cyanotype blueprint | The machine | The agent's world is engineered: ruler-straight, cold blue, precise |
| Comic panels | Babysitting the agent | Panels are squares: the page fills with repeated "continue? y" panels until it is a cage |
| Flipbook | The "y" cycles | The same moment, redrawn over and over |
| Techno flyer / risograph | The drop | The playlist's world: lavender halftone ink slams onto the pencil, misregistered on the kick |
| Continuous-line drawing | Looper | One line, never lifted: the loop that never breaks |
| Watercolour | Dawn | Colour arrives only once they are free |

## Colour is rationed

The film is graphite and blueprint, with **one lavender glint** in the glass orb, planted in the first wide
shot and never explained. Lavender spreads only after the choice. Amber watercolour arrives at dawn.

## Shape

The square ■ (cursor, gate, pane, panel, window) is the cage. The circle ● (orb, ring, sun, lens) is the loop.
The last image rounds the first image's square into the Looper orb.

## Sound

The score from v2 (derived from the playlist). Paper foley is added inside the techno: pencil scratch while drawings
draw themselves, eraser rub at each stop, a page flip on the comic cuts, and the pencil lead **snapping**
on the last futile "y".
