# "Keep the Loop" — director's treatment (v2, 90s, 48 bars at 128 BPM)

## Spine (McKee)

- **Protagonist:** a developer who has to ship a launch by 09:00.
- **Conscious desire:** get the work done tonight.
- **Unconscious need:** stop being the loop, the human who presses "y" all night.
- **Antagonism:** the Stop. Every coding agent halts and waits for a human to say "continue".
- **Controlling idea:** the work survives the night when you stop being the loop.
- **Value at stake:** captivity ↔ freedom (the desk vs. the night).
- **Gap:** each "y" should buy momentum. It buys one more stop, sooner.
- **Crisis choice:** keep babysitting four sessions with three hours left, or hand the loop off and get up.
  The choice is made with a hand on the menu bar and then with the whole body: leaving the chair.

## Visual grammar: every element has a job

| Element | Meaning | Where it pays off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Square ■** | The Stop: cursor, gate, notification, window pane, the grid that becomes a cage | Morphs into a circle at the drop, and again in the end card |
| **Circle ●** | The Loop: the glass orb, the ring track, the sun, the lens | Closing lens reflection; the end-card mark |
| Cold screen white | Obligation | Turns lavender when Looper takes over |
| Lavender | Looper | The orb, the ring, the room at the climax |
| Amber | Freedom, morning | Dawn floods the empty desk |
| The chair | Occupied = captive | Spinning empty = triumph |
| Wall clock, sticky "LAUNCH 9:00" | The stakes, shown and never said | Sunlight on the note at 08:52 |
| Rain | The night grinding on | Stops when they fall asleep (time has escaped them) |
| Window grid shadow on the floor | The room itself is a cage of squares | At dawn the same grid holds a round sun |
| A lit window across the street with someone at a desk | Everyone is stuck in this loop | Dark at dawn: they've gone to bed too |

Two worlds are cross-cut:
- **THE ROOM:** human scale, practical light, slow.
- **THE MACHINE:** the agent's world. A light racing a track through pylons, halted at square gates. It is fast, cold, and geometric, the playlist made visible.

## Sound design is story

The score's groove is the agent's momentum. When an agent stops, **the music stops**: a tape-stop into silence.
Each "y" restarts it, weaker, and every stop comes sooner. The audience feels the babysitting.
The crisis is a bar of silence broken only by the mouse click. The click lands on the downbeat of the drop.
